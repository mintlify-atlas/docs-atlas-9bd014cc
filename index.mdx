Robotic Arm Macondo is an open-source hardware project that lets you build a fully articulated, 3D-printed robotic arm with a functional claw — all controllable from your smartphone. The arm uses a 4-bar linkage mechanism, 13 custom-printed structural parts, and an Arduino UNO R4 shield driving multiple servo motors.

## What You'll Build

Robotic Arm Macondo is a multi-axis robotic arm with a pincer claw, driven by servo motors and controlled wirelessly from a smartphone app. The design features a 4-bar linkage for smooth, mechanically stable arm movement and a spur-gear claw mechanism for reliable gripping.

## Quick Start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Gather your parts">
    Review the [Bill of Materials](/bill-of-materials) and order hardware from Arduino Store, McMaster-Carr, and ServoCity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Print all 13 components">
    Follow the [3D Printing Guide](/3d-printing) to print every structural part from the provided STL files.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the electronics shield">
    Use the [KiCad schematic](/electronics/schematic) to assemble the Arduino UNO R4 servo driver shield.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assemble and connect">
    Follow the [Assembly Guide](/assembly) to put the arm together, then set up [smartphone control](/electronics/smartphone-control) to start operating it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  All CAD files are available in both STL (for slicing) and STEP (for modification) formats. The full OnShape model is publicly accessible at [cad.onshape.com](https://cad.onshape.com/documents/c667b97a3a2d7a4859eb4271/w/a18409ec0f56e84081c11f96/e/3d0234714fafda30fd940069).
